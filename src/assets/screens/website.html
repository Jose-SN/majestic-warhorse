The product

An online school loop you can run today

PetaxAI Learning helps communities and schools onboard people, deliver courses, assess learning, and support students with AI Mode — under their own organization identity.

  • Organizations

    Create a tenant, approve members, manage directory and assignments, customize branding.

  • Teachers

    Upload courses with chapters and media, build questionnaires, review answers, give feedback.

  • Students

    Join a school space, open courses, submit assessments, and ask questions in AI Mode.

Who it’s for

Built for schools and communities that teach together

Designed for organizations that need a clear school loop — not a content marketplace.

Schools & academies

Approve teachers and students, assign relationships, and keep courses in one place.

Churches & Sunday schools

Run structured learning for your community with simple membership controls.

Training teams

Deliver internal courses, collect answers, and coach learners with shared library context.
