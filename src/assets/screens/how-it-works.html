How it works now

The Class MVP school loop from signup to feedback.

1

Sign up or sign in

Create an account (email or Google). Identity is handled through PetaxAI IAM.

2

Choose your organization

Join a school space. Teachers and students may wait for admin approval.

3

Teach or learn with courses

Teachers upload courses and questionnaires; students open lessons and submit answers.

4

Teacher feedback

Teachers review submissions and leave feedback. Assessment is human-led.

5

AI Mode support

Ask study questions grounded in library and course content.

How can I use it now?

Pick your role for a short starter checklist. Browser only — no desktop install.

  1. Sign in as a student Create an account, join an organization, and open the app.
  2. Open courses & assessments Learn from teacher-assigned courses and submit answers.
  3. Use AI Mode for study help Ask questions grounded in library and course content.
  4. Read teacher feedback Review marks and comments on your submissions.
  1. Sign up as Teacher Join an organization and wait for approval if needed.
  2. Upload a course Create a course with chapters, videos, and attachments.
  3. Add a questionnaire Build questions students will answer for assessment.
  4. Review Answers Select a submission, leave feedback, and submit a review.
  1. Register as Organization Create the school account and land on your dashboard.
  2. Open Approvals Approve teachers and students waiting to join.
  3. Use Directory Assign teachers to students.
  4. Customize branding Set app name, logo, and colors for your org.
